Alongside my consulting work, I have started teaching professional courses in cooperation with the Akademie für Beruf & Karriere.

The courses focus on the practical use of digitalization, automation, and artificial intelligence in everyday work. They connect concrete use cases and implementation approaches with the foundations of responsible AI use and the relevant requirements of the EU AI Act.

I am delighted to continue sharing knowledge with professionals after several years of university teaching and to help participants translate new technologies into useful and responsible applications.
